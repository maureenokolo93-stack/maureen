# 20 Calm & Original Suno Prayer-Worship Prompts — November / Year-End Theme

**How to use in Suno (Custom mode):**
- Paste the **Style** text into *Style of Music*.
- Paste the **Lyrics** block into *Lyrics*.
- Suggested **Exclude Styles** for every song: `rap, EDM, heavy drums, distorted guitar, autotune, shouting`
- For longer videos, use **Extend** from the last chorus, or loop the instrumental under your own spoken prayer.

Every song uses its own Bible story, its own chorus wording and its own sound. None of them reuse the refrain or phrasing of the channel you're modelling.
